Frequently Asked Questions

How is the hourly fee calculated? +
The fee is clearly displayed on the card and map prior to confirmation. Billing starts when your vehicle entry is verified and stops upon departure.
What happens if my vehicle exceeds the garage size? +
Our flow rules automatically block reservations if your registered vehicle dimensions exceed the garage specifications set by the owner.
How do owners receive payments? +
Earnings accumulate in your "My Earnings" balance and can be transferred directly to your bank account via the platform.
